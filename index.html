Teaching Portfolio

Your portfolio, in one file.

Fill in what you have and drop in your photos and documents. Everything stays in this browser — nothing is uploaded. When you are happy, download a single HTML file that holds the portfolio, the slide deck and every image inside it. Email it, print it to PDF, or put it on any web host.

Your work is saved in this browser only. Clearing site data, or opening the builder on another device, starts from scratch. The downloaded file is the durable copy — it can be re-opened here later with Open a file.
